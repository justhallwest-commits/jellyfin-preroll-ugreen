How pre-rolls reach your devices

Adds a small script to the Jellyfin web app, so browsers, Jellyfin Media Player, the Android phone app and WebView TV apps queue a pre-roll before every movie and episode, including any episode you pick and every episode that autoplays. Refresh open Jellyfin tabs/apps after changing this.
For apps that don't use the web player (e.g. native TV apps): when a movie or episode starts without a pre-roll, the server tells the app to play the pre-roll first. You may see a split second of the episode first. Only works on apps that accept remote control.

Match pre-rolls to tags

Add tags to your pre-roll clips (clip → ⋮ → Edit metadata → Tags). A clip tagged Horror plays before movies or shows tagged Horror. Shows pass their tags down to their episodes. Clips with no tags are your general pre-rolls.
