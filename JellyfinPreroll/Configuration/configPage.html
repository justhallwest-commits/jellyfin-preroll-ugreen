How pre-rolls reach your devices

Browsers, Jellyfin Media Player, the Android phone app and WebView TV apps queue a pre-roll before every movie and episode, including autoplay. Reload open Jellyfin tabs and apps after changing this.
For apps without the web player (e.g. native TV apps): if something starts without a pre-roll, the server tells the app to play one first. Only for apps that accept remote control; there may be a split-second flash.
